FAQ

Q: How do I find Telegram bots?

Start with the categories in this list. To find groups, channels and videos, use the search bots Jisou (@jiso), SOSO (@soso) and Super Index (@CJSY).

Q: Which group management bot should I pick?

For full features choose Combot (@combot) or Rose (@MissRose_bot). For join captcha only, Shieldy (@shieldy_bot) or @P4CaptchaBot. Add Protectron (@ProtectronBot) if link ads are a problem.

Q: Are there free AI bots?

Yes. Every bot in the AI Bots section was verified as active, e.g. @GPT4Telegrambot (~5.08M monthly users). There is no official OpenAI Telegram bot; try small amounts before paying.

Q: Which bot downloads YouTube / Bilibili videos?

Use @freedlvideo_bot for multi-platform parsing, @utubest_Bot or @YtbDownBot for YouTube, and @bilibiliparse_bot for Bilibili. Do not download infringing content.

Q: Are Telegram bots safe?

Grant only needed permissions. Never enter passwords, verification codes or payment details in a bot chat. Be careful with bots asking for top-ups. Report abusive bots to @SpamBot.
